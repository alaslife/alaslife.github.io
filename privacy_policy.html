Privacy Policy for AlKeyboard

Last updated: August 21, 2026

App version: 1.0.0

AlKeyboard is a paid Android keyboard app published for Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, and what is sent only when you choose to use optional online features.

Product and Source Availability

AlKeyboard is distributed as a paid app through Google Play. The app is not offered as an open-source product, and this Privacy Policy website is provided only as the public privacy-policy page for users and Google Play review.

Summary

AlKeyboard is designed to be offline first. Core typing, text correction, themes, clipboard history, local dictionaries, haptics, and most settings work on your device. We do not run advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, keystroke logging, or background tracking in the app.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your keystrokes, typed text, clipboard content, contacts, personal dictionary, installed app names, images, device identifiers, precise location, payment card details, or analytics events.

Paid App Purchases

If you buy AlKeyboard on Google Play, your purchase, tax, refund, and payment information is handled by Google Play and Google Payments. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google may provide developers with limited order or transaction information needed to operate paid app distribution, refunds, and support.

Information Stored Locally

AlKeyboard may store the following information on your device:

  • App settings, themes, toolbar layout, and keyboard preferences.
  • Personal dictionary entries and learned words when you enable those features.
  • Clipboard history and pinned clipboard items when clipboard history is enabled.
  • Recent GIF or sticker history and pinned media items when you use media features.
  • Optional API keys that you enter for supported third-party services.
  • Debug logs only when you choose to export or share them.

This information is stored locally by the app or Android system storage. It is not uploaded to our servers.

Optional Online Features

AlKeyboard can connect to the internet only for features that need a network connection and that you choose to use. These features may include:

  • AI writing tools. Text that you explicitly submit to the AI tool may be sent to the configured AI provider, such as Google Gemini, to generate a response. Your API key is stored locally on your device.
  • GIF and sticker search. Search terms you enter in the media panel may be sent to the selected media provider, such as Klipy or another GIF provider, to return results.
  • Connection testing. If you use a connection test option, the app may contact a test endpoint to confirm that online features can reach the internet.

General typing outside these optional tools is not sent to these services by AlKeyboard.

Permissions

AlKeyboard may request Android permissions for the following purposes:

  • Internet and network state: Used for optional online features such as AI tools, GIF/sticker search, and connection testing.
  • Vibration: Used for haptic feedback while typing.
  • Contacts: Used only if you enable contact-name suggestions. Contact names are processed locally and are not uploaded by AlKeyboard.
  • User dictionary: Used to read or write words in Android's local user dictionary when you enable dictionary features.
  • Storage or media images: Used when you choose features that import files, show copied images, or include recent screenshots in clipboard history.
  • Boot completed: Used to keep keyboard services and dictionaries available after device restart.

Stickers and Sharing

When you send GIFs, stickers, copied images, logs, backups, or gesture data to another app, that receiving app handles the shared content under its own privacy policy. AlKeyboard cannot control what another app does after you choose to share data with it.

Gesture Data and Debug Exports

Some builds may include optional screens for gesture data collection, backup, restore, or log export. These files are created locally. They leave your device only if you choose to share, email, upload, or otherwise export them.

Children

AlKeyboard is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

AlKeyboard uses Android app storage and platform permission controls to protect local data. No method of storage or transmission is perfect, so you should only enable online features and share exports with services you trust.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes, especially when new features are added for the Play Store release. The updated policy will be posted at the same policy URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact option shown on the AlKeyboard Google Play listing.
